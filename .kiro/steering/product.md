# Product Overview

**Finexa** is a smart money tracking and financial management application that helps users take control of their finances through intelligent automation and insights.

## Core Features
- **Transaction Management**: Track income and expenses across multiple accounts
- **Budget Planning**: Create and manage budgets with intelligent recommendations  
- **Multi-Account Support**: Manage current and savings accounts in one place
- **Recurring Transactions**: Automated handling of recurring income/expenses
- **Smart Analytics**: AI-powered insights into spending patterns
- **Receipt Management**: Digital receipt storage and categorization

## Target Users
- Individual users seeking better financial control
- Small business owners managing business finances
- Freelancers tracking project-based income and expenses
- Anyone wanting automated financial insights and recommendations

## Key Value Propositions
- Automated transaction categorization and tracking
- Real-time financial insights and recommendations
- Simplified budget management with intelligent alerts
- Secure, user-friendly interface for financial data
